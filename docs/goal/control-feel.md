## One-line Goal
Give CL9's first-person controls measurable "손맛" — weight without sludge in the velocity curves, a camera that reads as a body (bob, roll, sprint FOV, landing spring on one clock with the footsteps), and no silent everyday verbs — without changing any balance number, verified by an automated PIE measurement against bands derived from Lethal Company and R.E.P.O.

## Background / Motivation
The owner: "the controls are not fun; I don't know how to get 손맛; compare with the installed Lethal Company / R.E.P.O. and make it feel similar; define a standard you genuinely consider good and build up to it" (2026-10-05, asked to decide without questions).

What recon found (2026-10-05, `docs/reference/` gets the full write-ups):
- CL9 today: walk 396 / run 654 cm/s, and every other movement value is the engine default (acceleration 2048, braking 2048, friction 8x2, air control 0.05 — confirmed on the `BP_CL9Character` CDO). Stop from a run takes about 0.11 s / 26 cm and releasing sprint about 0.025 s: effectively instant, no weight. No sprint ramp. The camera is rigid: FOV 90 fixed, no head bob, no sprint FOV, no roll, no landing response. Jump, landing, pickup, slot switch, flashlight, eating, cart grab and exhaustion make no sound. Combat feel (hit-stop, kick, sounds) was done in earlier passes and is not the complaint.
- Lethal Company (decompiled + serialized prefab values): walk 460, sprint 1035 after a 2.3 s multiplier ramp, 0.1 s walk start/stop, 0.23 s sprint release, 0.42 s drift on a sprint turn, animation-driven head bob 3.8 cm (2 Hz) / 12 cm (3 Hz) with the footstep event on the bob trough, FOV 66 -> 68, landing dip 26 cm in 0.17 s on a 2.5 m character, raw mouse.
- R.E.P.O.: rigidbody with 0.38-0.42 s to 90% speed and ~0.3 s stops, coyote 0.25 s, jump buffer 0.25 s, bob +8.7 cm with roll, footsteps fired by the bob phase, sprint FOV +20 (vertical 70 base), strafe roll 2 deg, overshooting jump/land camera springs, short shake impulses on everyday verbs.

## The standard ("good" = all six hold, each one measurable)
- **S1 Immediate** — every input gives its first local feedback (motion, sound or HUD) in the same frame on the machine that pressed it.
- **S2 Weight without sludge** — velocity changes follow readable curves (fast start, short stopping slide, sprint builds and bleeds off) and none is slower than the references' slowest.
- **S3 The camera is the body** — speed, footfalls, strafing, jumping and landing are readable from camera motion alone; footstep sound and bob share one clock.
- **S4 Forgiveness** — a jump the player meant to make happens (coyote time, jump buffer) — stretch tonight.
- **S5 Nothing everyday is silent** — jump, land, pickup, slot switch, light toggle (then eat, cart, exhaustion) all make a sound.
- **S6 Safe** — top speeds, stamina and jump height do not change; aim and server judgement still read the control rotation only; movement stays client-predicted with no new correction source; a comfort slider scales camera motion to zero.

## Design Decisions (panel-settled — do not relitigate during implementation)
- **C1 Sprint ramp mechanism** — Predicted state float SprintRamp (0..1) inside UCL9CharacterMovement, advanced in UpdateCharacterStateBeforeMovement after the stamina block with predicate (bWantsToRun && !bExhausted && Velocity.SizeSquared2D() > KINDA_SMALL) re-evaluated after the stamina update. Up = ease-out quad advanced exactly (a = 1-sqrt(1-S); a = min(1, a + dt/SprintRampUpTime); S = 1-(1-a)^2), down = linear S -= dt/SprintRampDownTime; a time <= 0 means instant. GetMaxSpeed cap = Walk' + (Run' - Walk') * SprintRamp (Walk'/Run' include their stat bonuses), then the existing clamp, cart multiplier and knockdown/execute zero. Snapshot in SetMoveFor, restore in PrepMoveFor, roll back in CombineWith. Not replicated, not in the correction payload, no new compressed flag. IsRunning() stays flag-based. No GetMaxAcceleration, CalcVelocity or SprintAccel/SprintReleaseDecel.
  - Rationale: Verified: the engine scales input with GetMaxAcceleration at input time (CMC.cpp:8136) and re-reads it after CombineWith reverted Velocity (CMC.cpp:9018-9019, 13282), so a speed-dependent max acceleration turns 480/2600 into an analog modifier of 0.185 and brakes the player. The cap-state ramp is frame-rate independent (walk to 90% of sprint gain 0.583 s at 60-240 Hz, 0.600 at 30 Hz in my replica), does not re-ramp after turns or bumps, and is the same netcode class as the existing Stamina.
  - Rejected: Draft M1 (stateless SprintAccel via GetMaxAcceleration) and both re-tunings of it by the feel seat (Accel 2600/SprintAccel 480/SprintReleaseDecel 1100) and the scope seat (Accel 2100/SprintAccel 420/SprintReleaseDecel 1050): same broken mechanism, frame-rate dependent, re-ramps after every corner.
- **C2 Saved-move combining fix is step 0** — Add FSavedMove_CL9::CombineWith: call Super, then restore Movement->Stamina, bExhausted, SprintRamp (and CoyoteTimeRemaining if S1 lands) from static_cast<const FSavedMove_CL9*>(OldMove)->Saved*, and copy the same values into this move's own Saved* fields. CanCombineWith stays as is. This is the first section of the gameplay brief, before any feel work.
  - Rationale: Verified by code reading: SetMoveFor runs after the pending frame already integrated (CMC.cpp:8961), CombineWith only reverts transform/velocity and jump counters (13268-13298), PerformMovement then re-runs the merged delta (9024). At 144 fps three frames merge per send, so client stamina drains 2.0x the host's. Any new time-integrated predicted state would inherit the defect.
  - Rejected: Scope seat's implicit plan (no CombineWith; coyote cut for being saved-move code while still adding a ramp); doing feel first and treating corrections as a final check.
- **C3 Braking model and values** — Engine-native single pair set in ApplyCharacterDef: bUseSeparateBrakingFriction = true, BrakingFrictionFactor = 1, BrakingFriction = 3.5, BrakingDecelerationWalking = 1300. One override: ApplyVelocityBraking(DeltaTime, Friction, BrakingDeceleration) replaces Friction with FallingLateralFriction when MovementMode == MOVE_Falling, then calls Super. Sprint release is produced by the cap falling over SprintRampDownTime (braking authority 1300 + 3.5v >= cap fall rate 1032 cm/s^2, and the engine clamp at CMC.cpp:3918-3921 lands speed on the cap). Every other over-max case (knockdown, execute, cart, buff expiry) uses the same stop braking.
  - Rationale: My replica at 30/45/60/90/120/144/240 Hz: walk stop 0.200 s / 28.6-35.0 cm, sprint stop 0.278-0.300 s / 66-77 cm, knockdown stop 0.283-0.300 s. That is inside all four ORIGINAL draft stop bands and under the 88.5 cm corridor clearance the feel seat raised, so no band has to be bent. CMC.cpp:3914 confirms BrakingFriction would otherwise leak into Falling.
  - Rejected: Netcode seat 1.5/1775 (sprint slide 82-87 cm, at the clearance limit, and it required restating the walk band to 22-40); feel seat 3.0/1700 (0.25 s, loses the weight and sits below the draft floor); scope seat 3.0/1350 with a friction-0 coast branch (depends on the rejected mechanism); a custom two-regime braking integrator.
- **C4 Walk acceleration** — MaxAcceleration = Def->Accel = 2000 cm/s^2 set as a plain member in ApplyCharacterDef.
  - Rationale: 0 to 90% walk 0.183 s at 60-120 Hz (band 0.14-0.22), identical to today's feel, A5 pounce-dodge arithmetic untouched; a constant keeps AnalogInputModifier correct everywhere. Weight comes from stopping, the ramp and the camera.
  - Rejected: Draft/feel 2600 (0.150 s, snappier than today, band edge at 120 Hz); scope 2100 (no reason to differ from the verified value).
- **C5 Sprint steering weight** — In UpdateCharacterStateBeforeMovement write GroundFriction = Lerp(Def->GroundFriction (8), Def->SprintTurnFriction (5.0), SprintRamp). No CalcVelocity override. The turn acceptance band is restated as: velocity within 15 degrees in 0.20-0.30 s AND drift along the old heading <= 115 cm.
  - Rationale: Replica under the cap model: snap-90 at full sprint aligns in 0.233-0.250 s, drift 94-110 cm, min speed 544-549, back at 95% by the alignment time, mouse-steered corners cost nothing. 5.0 under the cap model reproduces the drift the feel seat wanted from 6.0 under the draft model. Continuous in a predicted variable, so no branch on the steady-walk speed.
  - Rejected: Netcode 3.5 (135 cm drift in 255 cm corridors, chosen only to hit the draft's 0.28 s floor); feel 6.0 (0.217 s, nearly stock); draft speed-threshold switch (threshold sits on the walk attractor).
- **C6 Air control** — AirControl = Def->AirControl = 0.32 (with Accel 2000 = 640 cm/s^2 lateral). Jump height 156 cm and running-jump reach unchanged; standing-jump forward reach becomes about 205-225 cm and is recorded in the report.
  - Rationale: The netcode value 0.32 x 2000 and the feel seat's 0.25 x 2600 are the same authority (640 vs 650), so the two seats did not actually disagree once Accel is 2000. Plain member, net-safe.
  - Rejected: 0.25 at Accel 2000 (500 cm/s^2, below the target row); keeping 0.05.
- **C7 Stamina and balance arithmetic** — Drain rule unchanged (binary while sprint is active and moving). CL9DefRegistry::CheckCharacterRelations gets the ramp terms: RampLoss = (Run-Walk)*SprintRampUpTime/3, ReleaseGain = (Run-Walk)*SprintRampDownTime/2, SprintBudget = Run*Drain - RampLoss, SustainedTiredSpeed = (Run*Drain + Walk*Regen - RampLoss + ReleaseGain)/(Drain+Regen). Tools/Balance/check_inequalities.py is owned by no role, so the lead applies the same two formulas after Gate A or lists it as a follow-up.
  - Rationale: No inequality flips (tired speed 503.7 vs raccoon 462, budget 3851 vs 624). Changing the drain predicate would change what '6 s of stamina' means.
  - Rejected: Drain only above walk speed, or drain scaled by the ramp.
- **C8 Camera architecture and composition order** — No new UCameraModifier. One owner-only function ACL9Character::UpdateViewFeel(float Dt), called in the locally-controlled part of Tick and replacing the block at CL9Character.cpp:825-834. (1) CameraBaseZ (new member, seeded in ApplyCharacterDef) = FInterpTo(CameraBaseZ, downed ? DownedZ : EyeZ, Dt, 6) - never read back from the component. (2) FeelWeight = FInterpTo(FeelWeight, (knocked down | dead | executed | death cam) ? 0 : 1, Dt, 10). (3) FeelZ = clamp(M * FeelWeight * (BobZ + SpringZ), -22, +5); FirstPersonCameraComponent relative location = (0, 0, CameraBaseZ + FeelZ) so arms, held item and headlamp follow rigidly. (4) ClearAdditiveOffset(); AddAdditiveOffset(FTransform(FRotator(0, 0, Roll)), FovKick) with Roll = clamp(StrafeRoll + BobRoll, -2.5, 2.5) * M * FeelWeight. (5) Existing focus/prompt, UpdateStaminaShake (its scale multiplied by M), UpdateHitShakes unchanged; HurtLook and shake modifiers keep running after GetCameraView. M = UCL9GameUserSettings::CameraMotion.
  - Rationale: Verified: arms are children of the camera component (CL9Character.cpp:120), so POV-only translation makes them slide; additive offset is applied in GetCameraView and leaves FirstPersonFOV at 70 (CameraComponent.cpp:448-475). It is scoped to this camera by construction, so death cam, execute cam and spectating need no gate code. The existing knockdown sink reads the component Z back each tick (CL9Character.cpp:831-833), which would fight any added offset - hence CameraBaseZ.
  - Rejected: Draft M3 (everything in one POV-only modifier); scope seat's hybrid (translation on the component plus a new modifier for roll/FOV - a second lifecycle and view-target gating for nothing).
- **C9 Locomotion never offsets POV pitch or yaw** — Locomotion contributes only vertical translation, roll and FOV. Bob yaw, landing pitch nod, turn tilt, lateral bob and idle breathing are cut.
  - Rationale: Roll and FOV keep the screen centre on the control-rotation aim line (judgement reads GetControlRotation only); pitch/yaw stay the exclusive language of combat feedback. Idle breathing duplicates the existing stamina shake.
  - Rejected: Draft bob yaw +-0.4 deg and breathing; R.E.P.O.-style pitch/tilt.
- **C10 One clock for footsteps and bob** — Replace FootstepDistanceAccum with normalized StepPhase in [0,1): StepPhase += Moved / Stride, Stride = Lerp(FootstepStrideWalk 150, FootstepStrideRun 196, clamp((GroundSpeed-Walk)/(Run-Walk), 0, 1)). On wrap: subtract 1, play Footstep (volume Lerp(0.7, 1.0, same alpha), existing pitch jitter), toggle step parity, StepCount++. Never reset on stride change. While not stepping (air, stopped, downed, dead) freeze the phase; after 0.25 s of not stepping set it to 0.5. Runs on every machine for every pawn as today; bob reads the phase on the owner only.
  - Rationale: Verified the current accumulator hard-resets on air/stop and on every walk-run flip (CL9Character.cpp:1036-1054), which would pop the bob and drop the cadence when a ramped sprint starts. Normalized phase makes cadence monotonic (2.64 to 3.34 steps/s) and puts the bob trough on the footfall by construction.
  - Rejected: Draft/scope 'bob phase = the existing accumulator'.
